Initial Django foundation for GPI ticketing system

Add Lieu, Service, LieuService, UserProfile, ProblemType, Ticket and
TicketHistory models with ticket workflow business methods, Django
admin configuration, PostgreSQL settings via python-decouple, and a
seed_demo_data management command with demo groups, users, and
sample tickets.
